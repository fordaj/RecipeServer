Instructions

  1. Preheat your waffle iron.
  2. In a bowl, whisk the eggs, milk, oil/butter, and vanilla.
  3. Add the of the dry waffle mix and stir until just combined (lumps are okay—don't overmix).
  4. Cook in a preheated waffle iron per manufacturer’s instructions (usually 3–5 minutes until golden).
  5. Serve warm with toppings like butter, syrup, fruit, or whipped cream.

Tips

  • Storage plate in oven at 180°F
  • Liberally brush butter onto waffle maker between waffles
